Mia's prompt-based experiment aimed to produce 100 self-contained, visually distinct HTML works; the published collection includes **76 studies**. Original numbering skips 076–078. The original gallery and prompts are the source of truth. This BQ Design Critic catalog is a **linked reference index**, not a copy or fork of the creator's code and assets.

## How design agents should use these references

1. Start with the user's actual audience, task and brand requirements, not the gallery aesthetic.
2. Choose at most 2–3 **relevant** examples by interaction model, layout, material system or motion pattern.
3. Open the live demos and original prompts to understand the cause-and-effect, then independently sketch a different solution for the current project.
4. Borrow design principles, **not** code, imagery, trademarks, exact copy, or artwork. A public repo is not automatic permission to reuse its assets; verify rights separately.
5. Preserve information clarity, functional affordances, responsive layout, performance, accessibility and reduced motion.
6. **Never claim these studies have passed UX/accessibility tests.** The descriptions are source-derived; the featured notes below are our interpretation, not independently verified audits.
7. For transactional or information-dense products, prioritize task completion over elaborate creative scenes.
