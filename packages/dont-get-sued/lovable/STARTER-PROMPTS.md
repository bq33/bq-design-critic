**Review first**
> Activate the dont-get-sued skill and audit this current Lovable app for launch risks. Inspect actual code, screens and user flows; produce P0–P3 evidence-backed findings, prioritized technical fixes and questions for my lawyer. Do not change code, legal documents, consent gates or deployment yet.

**Privacy and tracking**
> Use Don't Get Sued to inventory analytics, advertising pixels, session replay and vendors. Test clean browser network behavior before consent, after Reject and after withdrawal where applicable; identify CCPA/GPC and EU/UK issues for counsel. Distinguish observed facts from jurisdictional applicability.

**Subscriptions**
> Audit trial and subscription price, billing cadence, renewal, affirmative consent, cancellation, refunds, Apple/Google policies and any California requirements. Use safe test mode, no real charges.

**Implement approved fixes**
> Take the reviewed technical P0/P1 findings and implement only approved reversible fixes. Preserve existing business rules, leave legal documents in draft until counsel approval, then report tested files, evidence and remaining unknowns.

**Lawyer handoff**
> Prepare a compact factual packet of policies, consent evidence, data inventory, third-party AI/SDKs, user age, marketing, subscriptions, UGC/IP and jurisdiction questions. Do not conclude the app is legally compliant.
